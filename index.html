About

On the Dot is an independent band from Boulder, Colorado. Their music is defined by a lively spirit, constant collaboration, and stylistic variety. The band was formed in 2022 through a shared passion of songwriting and live rock music. Since formation, the band has released two self-produced albums and has played numerous live shows across Colorado. The band is currently working on their next project and continues to play live shows thoughout Boulder.
